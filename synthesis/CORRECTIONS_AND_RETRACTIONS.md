# Corrections and Retractions

This register prevents attractive but invalid statements from silently returning to the mainline.

1. **Purity does not make ramification reduced.** Generic coefficient is `e-1`, not automatically `1`.
2. **Boundary abstractly isomorphic to A1 does not imply the normalization is smooth along it.** Quotient singularities may remain.
3. **Multiple inverse branches do not imply a global deck symmetry.** Non-Galois covers can have trivial deck group.
4. **Exactness of a top form does not force its divisor to vanish.** Exact forms may have zeros.
5. **The three-dimensional discriminant was overstated as a square.** The corrected factorization has a distinguished nonsquare factor times a square.
6. **No-affine-ramification does not imply a generic-fiber map is finite.** Finite asymptotic values are the precise loophole.
7. **Local monogenicity is not global monogenicity.** Moving index divisors can appear in the unramified locus.
8. **A primitive field element need not generate the integral closure.** Codimension-one and codimension-two index data matter.
9. **A torus degeneration need not preserve noninvertibility or geometric degree.** Sheets and boundary components may escape.
10. **The boundary-defect formula appeared in several incompatible forms.** It is not maintained as a theorem until its constructible-function hypotheses are rederived carefully.
11. **Low-degree frontiers conflict in the logs.** Numbers 5, 6, and 7 all appear; none may be used before primary-source audit.
12. **The one-boundary `K_Y=D` argument is not accepted.** It needs proof of ramification index two and control of singular points.
13. **A polynomial sl2 triple need not integrate algebraically.** The affine translation radical and completeness matter.
14. **The three-dimensional construction does not imply that every counterexample must have a comparable symmetry.** It supplies mechanisms and test cases only.
15. **Large validation or mutation counts do not increase theorem strength.** Structural checks are process evidence, not mathematical proof.
