# Track H — Monodromy, Galois Closure, and Cusp Braids

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

Over the complement of the nonproperness curve, a counterexample gives a connected finite étale cover. The cover need not be Galois and may have no deck transformations; its Galois closure carries the unavoidable finite symmetry.

### Candidate constraints

- every component meridian has a surviving affine sheet;
- transitive abelian monodromy with fixed points is impossible;
- purely nodal boundary configurations may therefore be excluded;
- remaining monodromy must be generated by cusp, tangency, or higher-collision braids.

### Open burden

Use polynomiality, exact log volume, and the single-tree boundary model to eliminate the remaining nonabelian braid data.

### Exit

Show the monodromy image cannot be both transitive and compatible with all boundary fixed-sheet and symplectic constraints.
