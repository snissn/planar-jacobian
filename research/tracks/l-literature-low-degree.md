# Track L — Literature and Low-Degree Audit

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

The conversations rely on many external theorems whose names, degree notions, and hypotheses were recalled inconsistently. This track is a prerequisite control, not exposition.

### Audit inventory

- Orevkov, Domrina, Żołądek: geometric/topological degree restrictions;
- Miyanishi and Gurjar–Miyanishi: group actions, normalization boundaries, sphere-like surfaces;
- Wright and Kaliman: simple polynomials, one-boundary models, irreducible fibers;
- Regeta: Lie-algebra reformulations;
- Jelonek: nonproperness sets;
- Borisov: compactification and boundary graphs;
- monogenic-generator and affine-plane-fibration literature.

For every theorem record exact bibliographic data, statement, field, smoothness, finiteness, degree notion, and whether it is published, preprint, corrected, or superseded.
