# Track I — Exact Symplectic Boundary Constraints

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

In two dimensions `dP∧dQ=dx∧dy`, and `P dQ + y dx` is a polynomial exact one-form. The graph is an exact algebraic Lagrangian.

### Potential leverage

- zero logarithmic residues along boundary divisors;
- constraints on leading terms of source coordinates under boundary valuations;
- compatibility with the canonical divisor/different;
- conductor moment conditions at cusp-like points.

### Warning

Residue cancellation does not eliminate higher-order poles. Exactness alone does not make the canonical divisor vanish.

### Exit

Derive a theorem that the complete principal-part package is incompatible with a nonempty normalization boundary.
