# Track D — Stable Differential Lattice

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

Seek a finite locally free `B=C[P,Q]`-order `M` in the function field that is preserved by both `D_P` and `D_Q`.

If such an order exists, its trace discriminant ideal is stable under both partial derivatives. The only nonzero derivative-stable ideal of `C[P,Q]` is the unit ideal, so the order is finite étale and the extension is trivial.

### Obstruction

Ordinary translations lift with poles at ramification divisors. Repeated differentiation may create unbounded pole order, while logarithmic vector fields preserve only logarithmic lattices.

### Candidate constructions

- saturate the normalization by bounded pole orders and prove stabilization;
- use duality/different ideals to choose a canonical lattice;
- combine the two derivations with boundary equations to cancel residues;
- use characteristic-p reductions to bound differential growth.

### Exit

Exhibit one finite stable order with exact proof of finite generation and invariance.
