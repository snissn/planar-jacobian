# Three-Dimensional Marked-Root Context

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

The announced three-dimensional map is retained only as a mechanism library. Its clean interpretation is a cubic with a marked simple root, equivalently a selected linear factor whose complementary quadratic factor is normalized to resultant one. Forgetting the mark is generically three-to-one. Repeated roots remove factorizations at infinity without finite critical points.

**Planar inspiration:** study sheet loss, boundary normalization, monodromy versus deck symmetry, weighted degenerations, and incidence varieties.

**Forbidden inference:** no theorem says that every planar counterexample must be marked-root, degree three, equivariant, or globally symmetric.
