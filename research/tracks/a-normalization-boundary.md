# Track A — Finite Normalization and Boundary

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

Let `B=C[P,Q]`, `L=C(x,y)`, and `C` be the integral closure of `B` in `L`. The primary object is `Y=Spec(C)` and the open immersion of the source.

### Established/candidate foundations

- Zariski Main finite factorization.
- Normal-surface Cohen–Macaulay and finite-flat package, subject to exact hypotheses.
- Generic ramification coefficient `e-1`.
- Boundary class-group localization argument.

### Open burdens

1. Bind the exact Gurjar–Miyanishi/Wright boundary statements to primary sources.
2. Separate smooth boundary, quotient-singular boundary, and codimension-two collisions.
3. Reprove any Euler/defect formula from constructible local lengths rather than reuse a conversational version.
4. Determine which boundary statements survive without assuming irreducibility or reduced ramification.

### Exit

Show the finite map is unramified in codimension one and has no codimension-two nonproperness; purity and finite-étale triviality then finish.
