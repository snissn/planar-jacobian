# Track E — Quasi-Albanese and Log Geometry

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

Delete two generic fibers `p=P-alpha` and `q=Q-beta`. The resulting open surface has unit lattice expected to be generated by `p,q`, so its intrinsic quasi-Albanese map is the restriction to `(G_m)^2`.

If this map is finite, the character-lattice index should force degree one. Therefore a counterexample must make the quasi-Albanese nonproper.

### Work packages

- prove the unit-lattice statement with exact irreducibility hypotheses;
- compute log plurigenera and log Kodaira dimension from a controlled compactification;
- show the nonproperness divisor contradicts logarithmic Chern or canonical constraints;
- compare with the finite normalization rather than only the original source.

### Exit

Prove quasi-Albanese finiteness or a theorem that its nonproperness cannot occur for a Keller pair.
