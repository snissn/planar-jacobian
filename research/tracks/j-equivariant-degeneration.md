# Track J — Equivariant Degeneration

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

The three-dimensional example shows how a residual torus can organize a nonproper étale cover. In the plane, exactly equivariant Keller maps appear rigid, suggesting a degeneration-to-symmetry strategy.

### Missing no-escape theorem

A torus limit can become an automorphism, lower geometric degree, or discard a boundary valuation. A useful degeneration must remain inside a stratum with fixed function-field degree, boundary dual graph, valuation key sequences, and inertia data.

### Exit

Prove that a minimal counterexample has a closed torus orbit inside such a fixed stratum, then apply the equivariant no-go theorem.
