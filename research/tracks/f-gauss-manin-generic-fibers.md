# Track F — Gauss–Manin and Generic Fibers

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

The quotient `N=C[x,y]/D_Q(C[x,y])` models puncture cohomology on generic `P`-fibers. The de Rham complex in the `dP,dQ` frame gives a candidate proof that `D_P` acts bijectively on `N`.

A counterexample therefore needs a nonzero puncture module carrying an invertible transverse connection.

### Open burdens

- identify `N` with a finite regular connection under precise tameness hypotheses;
- exploit its origin in a polynomial algebra, not an arbitrary Kummer connection;
- rule out finite asymptotic values for `Q` on generic `P`-fibers;
- bind the surjective-derivation classification needed when `N=0`.

### Exit

Prove `N=0` or show that no nonzero module of this geometric origin admits the required bijective connection.
