# Track K — Characteristic p and p-Curvature

> Status: `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

Reduce a spread-out Keller pair and its commuting frame modulo good primes. Explore whether zero or constrained p-curvature, p-bases, or Frobenius behavior forces finite monodromy or a stable differential lattice.

### Risks

- inseparability and new units after reduction;
- Artin–Schreier phenomena with no characteristic-zero analogue;
- failure to lift a mod-p invariant uniformly;
- using density of good primes without an integral model.

### Exit

A prospective theorem over a finitely generated Z-algebra that returns a characteristic-zero contradiction, not isolated finite-field evidence.
