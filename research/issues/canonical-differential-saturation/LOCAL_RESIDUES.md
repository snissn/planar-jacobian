# Local Fractional Residues and Differential Escape

> **Claims:** local proof of `CDS-002`, `CDS-004`, `CDS-005`  
> **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## 1. Tame Kummer calculation

Let \(R\) be a characteristic-zero DVR with uniformizer \(t\), and
let a totally ramified factor be

\[
S=R[s]/(s^e-t),\qquad e>1.
\]

For the normalized transverse derivation \(\delta(t)=1\), the unique
extension to the fraction field satisfies

\[
D(s)=\frac{1}{es^{e-1}}=\frac1e t^{-1}s.
\]

For \(0\le j<e\),

\[
tD(s^j)=\frac je s^j.
\]

Thus the associated-graded residue classes are

\[
\left\{0,\frac1e,\ldots,\frac{e-1}{e}\right\}
\pmod{\mathbf Z}.
\]

If the residue degree is \(f\), strict-henselian splitting produces
\(f\) copies of this multiset.

## 2. Repeated derivatives

For \(N\in\mathbf Z\) and \(0<j<e\),

\[
D^n(t^Ns^j)
 =
\left[
\prod_{r=0}^{n-1}
\left(N+\frac je-r\right)
\right]t^{N-n}s^j.
\]

Every factor is nonzero in characteristic zero.  With \(v(t)=1\),

\[
v\!\left(D^n(t^Ns^j)\right)=N-n+\frac je
\longrightarrow-\infty.
\]

Any full finite \(R\)-lattice is commensurable with \(S\), so it
contains \(t^NS\) for some \(N\).  It therefore contains a
nontrivial character \(t^Ns^j\), and its differential saturation is
valuation-unbounded.

## 3. General transverse derivation

If \(\delta(t)=a\in R^\times\), then

\[
D(s)=\frac{a}{e}t^{-1}s.
\]

Repeated differentiation gives the same falling-factorial expression
times \(a^n\) as the unique lowest-valuation term.  Terms involving
derivatives of \(a\) have higher valuation.  Consequently no
cancellation removes the escape.

A change of tame parameter or of uniformizer shifts residue
representatives by integers or conjugates the factor.  The class
multiset is intrinsic.

## 4. Tangential derivations

If \(\delta(t)\in(t)\), write \(\delta(t)=tb\).  In a tame equation
\(t=us^e\),

\[
e\frac{D(s)}s=b-\frac{D(u)}u
\]

is regular.  The normalization is preserved.  Thus one fixed
derivation admits a stable full lattice exactly when it is
logarithmic along every ramified divisor.

For the canonical pair, an irreducible reduced branch equation
\(h(P,Q)\) cannot divide both \(h_P\) and \(h_Q\).  Hence one member
of the pair is transverse at its generic point.

## 5. Pair spectrum

At \(h=us^e\), unit terms are regular and the \(j\)-th character has
pair residue

\[
\frac je(\overline{h_P},\overline{h_Q}).
\]

In a normal/tangent frame this becomes

\[
(j/e,0).
\]

Integer twists shift only the first coordinate by an integer on each
fixed character line.  The two commuting residues are simultaneously
semisimple after tame splitting, but their commutativity supplies no
cancellation.

## 6. Tame non-Galois cubic

Consider

\[
z^3-3z-t=0.
\]

Its discriminant is \(27(4-t^2)\), so the generic cubic is
non-Galois.  Near \(t=2\), set \(z=-1+s\) and \(t=2+\tau\).  Then

\[
\tau=s^2(s-3),\qquad
D_t(s)=\frac{1}{3s(s-2)}.
\]

The ramified quadratic valuation factor has class \(1/2\) and
unbounded derivative escape.  The argument uses valuation factors,
not a global Galois action.

## 7. Singular branch curves

For a cusp branch

\[
h=p^2-q^3,\qquad s^e=h,
\]

the lifted translations satisfy

\[
D_p(s)=\frac{2p}{e h}s,\qquad
D_q(s)=-\frac{3q^2}{e h}s.
\]

At the generic point of the irreducible curve, the normal covector
\((2p,-3q^2)\) is nonzero, so the same \(j/e\) obstruction is present.
The cusp point itself is codimension two and cannot remove the
generic height-one class.

## 8. Conductor, different, and normalization effects

The inverse different in the Kummer model is generated by

\[
(es^{e-1})^{-1}.
\]

A trace-dual basis can be written as
\((1/e)s^{j+1-e}\).  Its residue representatives are

\[
\frac{j+1-e}{e},
\]

the same multiset modulo \(\mathbf Z\).

A base-divisor twist \(t^m\) shifts each fixed character
representative by the integer \(m\).  Multiplication by a fractional
ramification parameter \(s^k\), as in a different or canonical
fractional ideal, may additionally permute the tame character labels:

\[
\left\{\frac{j+k}{e}\bmod\mathbf Z:0\le j<e\right\}
 =
\left\{\frac je\bmod\mathbf Z:0\le j<e\right\}.
\]

Thus conductor powers, trace duals, inverse differents, canonical
twists, and rank-one reflexive fractional ideals preserve the full
intrinsic class multiset even when they do not act by one common
integer shift in a chosen basis.  Their reflexive hulls are determined
at height one and retain the same obstruction.

## 9. Ordinary versus logarithmic conclusion

The Kummer normalization is a finite coherent logarithmic lattice
preserved by \(tD_t\).  No full finite lattice is preserved by
\(D_t\).  Therefore the fractional-residue obstruction:

- rules out every ordinary coherent full lattice under a transverse
  derivation;
- permits coherent logarithmic lattices;
- survives combining all sheets, reflexive hull, completion, strict
  henselization, conductor shifts, fractional-ideal twists, and global
  monodromy;
- is diagnostic, not contradictory, until the polynomial Keller
  source supplies an ordinary coherent extension.
