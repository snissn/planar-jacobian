## Disposition

This packet does **not** prove that every rank-three Keller normalization has
degree one. It proves a rank-three foundation package, corrects a false
surrogate for the unit-value problem, extracts two Keller-specific structural
facts, and reduces the surviving obstruction to explicit boundary and
coefficient identities.

The exact status is:

> `BLOCKED`: the universal binary cubic can have unit coefficient/content ideal
> while representing no unit. Keller étaleness forces the normal different away
> from the specified source and supplies a finite source-derived codimension-one
> generator certificate, but it does not yet eliminate a moving unramified
> collision divisor for one integral section.

The packet therefore realizes acceptable dispositions 3 and 5 from the task:

1. the theorem is reduced to a smaller boundary-cube/divisor identity together
   with an exact cubic differential identity; and
2. the proposed implication “universal index ideal is the unit ideal, therefore
   one section has unit index” is false.

## Setup and ring direction

Let

```text
B = C[P,Q],  K = Frac(B),  L = C(x,y),
O = normalization of B in L,  Y = Spec(O),
U = Spec(A),  A = C[x,y].
```

The Zariski-Main open immersion is `U -> Y`. Its ring map has the direction

```text
O -> A.
```

All source functions are therefore rational functions on `Y`, regular on `U`;
they are not silently assumed integral over `B` on all of `Y`.

The rank-three work is conditional on the explicitly stated hypothesis that
`O` is finite locally free of rank three over `B`. The issue #3 finite-flatness
audit is rechecked in [`INDEX_FORM_FOUNDATIONS.md`](INDEX_FORM_FOUNDATIONS.md).

## Main scoped results

- `IDX3U-01`: trace splitting gives `O=B*1 direct_sum E`, with `E` projective
  of rank two; Quillen-Suslin makes `E` free over `C[P,Q]`.
- `IDX3U-02`: `Phi(s)=1 wedge s wedge s^2` is an intrinsic section of
  `det(O)=det(E)`. In a frame of `E` it is a binary cubic, and
  `B_p[s]=O_p` exactly when its localized value is a unit.
- `IDX3U-03`: the universal coefficient/content ideal is the zeroth Fitting
  ideal of the cokernel of `Sym^3(E)->det(E)` and equals the ideal generated by
  all values. It is frame-independent. Its being `B` does **not** imply a unit
  value; the issue #3 cubic already has coefficient `1` and no unit value.
- `IDX3U-04`: for each canonical target derivation there is a nonzero clearing
  denominator `h_D in B` with `h_D D(O) subset O`, and consequently
  `h_D partial_D(J_Phi) subset J_Phi`. No unweighted derivative stability, and
  no canonical discriminant multiplier, is proved.
- `IDX3U-05`: every source function can be made integral by a base multiplier
  supported on images of boundary divisors. For a source function `r`,
  `Phi(m r)=m^3 Phi_K(r)`. Four fixed source directions separate every
  rank-three fiber away from the closed boundary image, but only as a finite
  collection.
- `IDX3U-06`: for any primitive cubic coordinate `t`, writing
  `x=X(t), y=Y(t)` gives an exact coefficient identity forced by
  `dP wedge dQ=dx wedge dy`; all coefficient denominators remain visible.
- `IDX3U-07`: Keller étaleness forces the ramification/different support into
  `Y-U`. This excludes the issue #3 model's invariant failure. If one target
  coordinate is affine-linear on its displayed source plane, every
  constant-Jacobian repair is an automorphism and cannot retain degree three.
- `IDX3U-08`: the remaining bridge is to turn `IDX3U-05` and `IDX3U-06` into
  one integral section `s` with `Phi(s) in C*`.
