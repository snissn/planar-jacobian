## External theorem bindings

1. **Reflexive modules on regular surfaces.**  Stacks Project, Tag `0B3N`:
   a coherent reflexive module on a regular scheme of dimension at most two
   is finite locally free.
2. **Maximal Cohen--Macaulay over a regular local ring.**  Stacks Project,
   Tag `00NT`: a maximal Cohen--Macaulay module over a regular local ring is
   free.
3. **Purity of the branch locus.**  Stacks Project, Lemma `0BMB`, within
   Section `0BJE`: for a finite generically etale map to a regular local base,
   the relevant branch locus cannot be supported only in codimension at
   least two under the stated normality hypotheses.
4. **Rank-one reflexive modules and Weil divisors.**  Stacks Project, Tag
   `0EBM`: rank-one coherent reflexive modules on a normal scheme correspond
   to divisor classes.
5. **Open-immersion pole colimit.**  Stacks Project, Tag `0EBN`: direct images
   across a codimension-one complement are expressed as a colimit of
   reflexive divisorial twists.
6. **Finite normalization and reflexivity.**  Stacks Project, Tag `0BM4`:
   finite integral closures over a normal base have the stated reflexivity
   behavior under the finite separable hypotheses.
7. **Derivations and etale maps.**  Stacks Project, Tags `00UP` and `0H94`:
   derivations extend uniquely through etale maps.
8. **Tame DVR forms.**  Stacks Project, Tags `09E9`, `09EV`, `0EXT`, and
   `0EYF`: tame ramification reduces after unramified base change to a root of
   a uniformizer.
9. **Connected finite etale covers of complex affine space.**  SGA 1,
   Expose XII, Theorem 5.1, together with simple connectedness of `C^2`.

## Hypothesis boundary

The packet uses:

- characteristic zero;
- finite separable `L/K`;
- finite normalization;
- normality and dimension two for the divisorial intersection arguments;
- regularity of `B=C[P,Q]` for local freeness and purity;
- the specified source open only through `O->A` and source regularity of the
  canonical derivations.

No source is cited as an existence theorem for a finite stable lattice.
