## Source-binding rule

Before promoting a `LITERATURE` statement into a load-bearing proof dependency:

1. quote the exact theorem number and hypotheses;
2. bind the source version/date or commit;
3. verify that field, characteristic, smoothness, properness, finiteness, and normality assumptions match;
4. distinguish coordinate degree, topological degree, and function-field degree;
5. record whether the source proves a theorem, a reduction, a conditional statement, or a computational exclusion.
