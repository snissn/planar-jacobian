# Research Program

## Goal

Prove that every polynomial map `F=(P,Q): A2_C -> A2_C` with nonzero constant Jacobian is an automorphism.

## Critical path

The common reduction is to the finite normalization of `C[P,Q]` in `C(x,y)`. The affine source embeds as an open subset. If the finite normalization is étale over the target, it is trivial; therefore all difficulty is carried by the deleted boundary and its codimension-two collisions.

## Parallel proof families

### Algebraic globalization

- global primitive element;
- elimination of unramified index divisors;
- finite stable differential order;
- global complete-intersection or power-basis purity.

### Dynamical and Lie-theoretic globalization

- local finiteness/completeness of canonical translation, Euler, or radial fields;
- rational integration of the canonical affine action;
- elimination of boundary poles.

### Fiber and logarithmic geometry

- Gauss–Manin puncture module;
- quasi-Albanese finiteness;
- log plurigenus and log-general-type constraints;
- exact symplectic residues and principal parts.

### Boundary models and topology

- Wright one-boundary graded reduction;
- single-tree Poisson rigidity;
- cusp/tangency braid elimination;
- Galois closure and inertia constraints.

### Transformative routes

- equivariant degeneration without loss of boundary data;
- characteristic-p and p-curvature.

## Exit rule

A branch exits successfully only when it supplies a complete implication from the Keller hypothesis to degree one, with all imported theorems source-bound and all local-to-global steps explicit. A restricted theorem is banked at its exact scope and does not become a family-wide no-go.
