# Research Program

## Goal

Prove that every polynomial map `F=(P,Q): A2_C -> A2_C` with nonzero constant Jacobian is an automorphism.

## Critical path

The common reduction is to the finite normalization of `C[P,Q]` in `C(x,y)`. The affine source embeds as an open subset. If the finite normalization is étale over the target, it is trivial; therefore all difficulty is carried by the deleted boundary and its codimension-two collisions.

No branch below is presently a proof of `JC_2`. Each supplies either a sufficient bridge, a restricted theorem, or an obstruction that must be integrated with the boundary geometry.

## Parallel proof families

### Algebraic globalization

- global primitive element;
- elimination of unramified index divisors;
- finite stable differential order;
- global complete-intersection or power-basis purity.

### Dynamical and Lie-theoretic globalization

- local finiteness/completeness of canonical translation, Euler, or radial fields;
- rational integration of the canonical affine action;
- elimination of boundary poles.

### Fiber and logarithmic geometry

- Gauss–Manin puncture module;
- quasi-Albanese finiteness;
- log plurigenus and log-general-type constraints;
- exact symplectic residues and principal parts.

### Boundary models and topology

- Wright one-boundary graded reduction;
- single-tree Poisson rigidity;
- cusp/tangency braid elimination;
- Galois closure and inertia constraints.

### Transformative and filtered routes

- exact `G_m`-equivariant rigidity, source-bound to Shaska's arXiv:2607.20210;
- weighted Rees deformation and its staircase equations;
- filtered reduction toward an exact graded model;
- equivariant degeneration without loss of function-field degree or boundary data;
- characteristic-p and p-curvature.

The active filtered leaf is [`L13-defect-4-staircase.md`](leaf-packets/L13-defect-4-staircase.md). It treats the claimed low-defect reduction as a candidate and isolates the first new middle-Wronskian obstruction at defect `4`.

## Cross-track interfaces

The filtered-equivariance lane must not remain a formal weight calculation detached from geometry. A successful reduction should identify at least one of the following:

- a monotone Newton/valuation invariant on the finite-normalization boundary;
- a filtration-compatible polynomial symplectic transformation;
- a cusp or inertia configuration excluded by the monodromy track;
- a no-escape result preserving the boundary stratum under degeneration.

Conversely, a formal defect-4 layer system that survives all staircase equations but cannot come from a global polynomial pair would identify the precise additional realization theorem required.

## Exit rule

A branch exits successfully only when it supplies a complete implication from the Keller hypothesis to degree one, with all imported theorems source-bound and all local-to-global steps explicit. A restricted theorem is banked at its exact scope and does not become a family-wide no-go.

For the defect-4 leaf, an exact scoped obstruction or formal counterexample is also a successful scientific disposition; it does not by itself close the main goal.
