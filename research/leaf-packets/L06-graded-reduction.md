# Wright Graded Reduction

- **Priority:** `P0`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-024, CLM-025
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Reduce an arbitrary one-boundary Keller pair to a homogeneous nonzero constant-bracket pair.

## Accepted evidence

A terminating valuation/Newton procedure preserving the decisive bracket term.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not replace algebraic dependence of leading forms by polynomial dependence without proof.

## Required artifacts

Reduction invariant, termination measure, multi-valuation compatibility, and final homogeneous pair.

## Stop rule

Stop when the declared implication is proved at exact scope, or when a minimal counterexample to the proposed bridge is constructed. Bank useful restricted lemmas separately; do not widen the leaf to the entire conjecture without a graph update.

## Handoff

Record exact formulas, source bindings, tested countermodels, open sublemmas, and the smallest next action.
