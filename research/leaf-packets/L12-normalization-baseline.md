# Normalization Baseline Audit

- **Priority:** `P0`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-003–CLM-007
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Establish a reviewed baseline for quasi-finiteness, the finite-normalization factorization, finite flatness, boundary shape, class group, and generic canonical-divisor coefficients.

## Accepted evidence

Self-contained proofs where short, and exact primary-source bindings where substantial classification is used.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not assume `K_Y` is the reduced boundary, that all ramification indices are two, or that an abstract affine-line boundary makes `Y` smooth.

## Required artifacts

A theorem packet with hypotheses, singular cases, degree conventions, exact statements, proof/source audit, and updates to every dependent graph node.

## Stop rule

Stop only when the baseline is independently reviewable and every disputed conversational version is either corrected or retired.

## Handoff

Record source theorem numbers, local computations, unresolved singular cases, and downstream claims that remain blocked.
