# Primary-Source Audit

- **Priority:** `P0`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-003–CLM-005, CLM-015–CLM-017, CLM-021, CLM-044
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Bind every imported theorem used by the proof graph to its exact primary-source statement and resolve conflicting low-degree and boundary claims.

## Accepted evidence

Primary papers, official preprints, published corrections, and exact theorem numbers with verified hypotheses.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not use search-result summaries, secondary expositions, or conversational recollections as theorem authority.

## Required artifacts

A source ledger recording authors, title, version, theorem, field, degree notion, smoothness/normality assumptions, corrections, and supported claim IDs.

## Stop rule

Stop only when every literature-dependent blocker has an exact source disposition: verified, corrected, weakened, or retired.

## Handoff

Record unresolved bibliographic conflicts, inaccessible sources, theorem wording, and the precise claim-ledger updates required.
