# Characteristic-p and p-Curvature

- **Priority:** `P3`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-045
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Construct an integral model and derive a uniform good-prime obstruction—through p-curvature, p-bases, Frobenius, or finite monodromy—that lifts to characteristic zero.

## Accepted evidence

A prospective theorem over a finitely generated integral base with a precise lifting argument.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not infer a characteristic-zero theorem from isolated finite-field computations or ignore Artin–Schreier and inseparability phenomena.

## Required artifacts

A spread-out model, excluded-prime set, reduced derivation identities, p-curvature calculation, counterexamples, and lifting theorem.

## Stop rule

Stop when a uniform arithmetic invariant gives a characteristic-zero contradiction or when a concrete mod-p phenomenon invalidates the proposed route.

## Handoff

Record the integral equations, primes tested, exact Frobenius identities, failure cases, and the smallest uniformity lemma still needed.
