# Gauss–Manin Puncture Vanishing

- **Priority:** `P1`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-020, CLM-021, CLM-035
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Prove C[x,y]/D_Q(C[x,y]) vanishes or cannot carry the required bijective D_P action.

## Accepted evidence

An exact algebraic or D-module theorem using polynomial-source structure.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not use regularity or acyclicity alone; Kummer connections are countermodels.

## Required artifacts

Finite generation and tameness proof, connection matrix, local exponents, and contradiction.

## Stop rule

Stop when the declared implication is proved at exact scope, or when a minimal counterexample to the proposed bridge is constructed. Bank useful restricted lemmas separately; do not widen the leaf to the entire conjecture without a graph update.

## Handoff

Record exact formulas, source bindings, tested countermodels, open sublemmas, and the smallest next action.
