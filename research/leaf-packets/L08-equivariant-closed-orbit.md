# Equivariant Closed Orbit

- **Priority:** `P2`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-016, CLM-046
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Find an equivariant degeneration of a minimal counterexample that preserves function-field degree, boundary type, and inertia data and reaches a closed torus orbit.

## Accepted evidence

A closed-orbit theorem inside a precisely defined invariant stratum, followed by application of the equivariant planar no-go theorem.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not use an ordinary coefficient limit: it may become an automorphism, lower degree, or discard a boundary valuation.

## Required artifacts

The algebraic parameter stratum, invariants held fixed, properness/no-escape proof, limiting symmetry, and the exact no-go implication.

## Stop rule

Stop when the fixed-stratum closed-orbit statement is proved or when an explicit degeneration demonstrates unavoidable escape. Do not promote a formal initial form to a counterexample.

## Handoff

Record the one-parameter subgroup, Rees algebra or compactification, preserved invariants, lost data, and the smallest missing semicontinuity theorem.
