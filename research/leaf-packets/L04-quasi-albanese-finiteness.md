# Quasi-Albanese Finiteness

- **Priority:** `P1`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-018, CLM-019
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Prove the intrinsic map from the two-fiber complement to (G_m)^2 is finite.

## Accepted evidence

A properness theorem based on log geometry or boundary valuations.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not assume maximal log irregularity, low log plurigenus, or unchanged unit lattice alone implies finiteness.

## Required artifacts

A compactification, divisor calculation, unit-lattice proof, and nonproperness exclusion.

## Stop rule

Stop when the declared implication is proved at exact scope, or when a minimal counterexample to the proposed bridge is constructed. Bank useful restricted lemmas separately; do not widen the leaf to the entire conjecture without a graph update.

## Handoff

Record exact formulas, source bindings, tested countermodels, open sublemmas, and the smallest next action.
