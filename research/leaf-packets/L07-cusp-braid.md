# Cusp-Braid Elimination

- **Priority:** `P1`
- **Status:** `OPEN`
- **Dependencies:** CLM-026–CLM-028
- **Authority:** `MUTABLE_NONAUTHORITATIVE`

## Load-bearing question

Eliminate transitive nonabelian monodromy compatible with a fixed affine sheet for every boundary meridian and with the exact logarithmic volume form inherited from a Keller map.

## Accepted evidence

A theorem for the actual plane-curve complement and finite-normalization boundary, using polynomiality or exact log-symplectic data—not covering topology alone.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not assume component meridians commute. Do not assume the Galois closure is an affine plane or that its group action descends to the original source.

## Required artifacts

An inertia table, braid relations, source-polynomiality constraints, exact excluded configurations, and surviving singularity types.

## Stop rule

Stop when the declared implication is proved at exact scope, or when a minimal compatible nonabelian configuration is constructed. Bank restricted no-go results separately; do not claim family-wide closure from a nodal or low-complexity case.

## Handoff

Record the branch curve model, meridian generators, permutation action, log-volume constraints, tested braid factorizations, and the smallest next exclusion.
