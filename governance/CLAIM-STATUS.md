# Claim Status Vocabulary

- `verified_internal`: direct derivation checked from maintained definitions.
- `verified_conceptual`: logically stable background distinction, not a new theorem.
- `candidate_proved`: a proof is present in the synthesis but requires independent audit before authority.
- `candidate`: promising statement with incomplete proof or unverified hypotheses.
- `literature_bound`: intended to rest on an external theorem; exact source binding required.
- `source_audit_required`: conflicting or incomplete external attribution.
- `open_bridge`: explicitly unproved implication that would materially advance or close a branch.
- `speculative`: idea or experiment without a theorem packet.
- `retired`: false, overstrong, superseded, or withdrawn statement.

Only reviewed `verified_internal` claims and exact `literature_bound` claims may support a frozen theorem packet.
