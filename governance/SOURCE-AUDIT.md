# Source Audit Protocol

For every external theorem record:

- authors, title, venue/preprint version, year, and stable identifier;
- exact theorem number and statement;
- base field and characteristic;
- smoothness, normality, finiteness, properness, and degree conventions;
- whether corrections or errata exist;
- the precise claim-ledger entries it supports;
- a short note explaining why the hypotheses apply.

Do not cite a conversational recollection as authority. Prefer primary papers and official versions.
